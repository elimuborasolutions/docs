Elimu Bora is the school management platform that runs your school's day-to-day work in one place: admissions and staff records, the timetable and registers, assessments and report cards, fees and M-Pesa payments, events, sports, clubs, and the audit trail behind all of it. It is built for Kenyan schools running CBC or 8-4-4, and every school gets its own tenant on its own subdomain so its data stays separate from every other school's. This help center walks you through what each part of the system does, the workflows you run inside it, and what guardians see when they sign in.

## Who uses Elimu Bora

Three groups sign in to the panel. The work each group does is documented in the section linked beside it.

- **School leadership** (principals, deputies, directors) and **non-teaching staff** (bursars, registrars, HR, storekeepers) run admissions, finance, procurement, and school-wide oversight.
- **Teaching staff** (class teachers, subject teachers, heads of department) mark registers, set up assessments, enter grades, and run their timetables.
- **Guardians** sign in to a locked, read-only account scoped to their own children. They read attendance, report cards, invoices and payments, wallet and earmark balances, events their child is invited to, and the clubs and teams their child belongs to.

Students do not sign in to the system. Everything that concerns a student reaches their guardian instead, on the guardian's own login and in the notifications the guardian receives.

## How the platform is organised

Each school runs on its own subdomain at `<school>.elimuboraerp.com`. Signing in there lands you on the dashboard, and the sidebar groups the modules your role can reach. The platform is built around twelve modules:

- **Foundation modules** are always on for every school: Users & Identity, Curriculum, Academic Years & Terms, and Assessments & Report Cards. They define who is in the school, the academic structure those people work inside, the calendar that anchors everything to a term, and the assessments and report cards that close each term.
- **Operational modules** can be turned on per school in [School Settings](/settings): Attendance, Timetable & Lessons, Finance, Inventory & Procurement, Events, Sports, Clubs, and the Activity Log audit trail.

Your role decides what the sidebar shows and what each page lets you do. A class teacher sees the streams they own and the subjects they teach; a bursar sees the finance pages; a guardian sees their own children. Roles and the permissions inside them are documented under [Roles & Permissions](/roles-permissions).

## Using this help center

Every module page has the same shape, so once you know one you know them all:

- **Records you'll work with** names each record the module touches and what it means in plain English.
- **Workflow sections** are grouped by the area of work (for example, **Set up fees** or **Daily registers**) and lead with the goal. Each workflow opens with the prerequisites you need, then walks the steps.
- **Statuses and lifecycle** lists every status a record can be in, who can act in it, and a diagram of how the record moves from one status to the next.
- **Reports and analytics** lists the downloadable reports the module owns and where each one lives in the panel.
- **What guardians see** describes the same module from the guardian's side: what is visible, what is hidden, and what notifications reach them.
- **FAQs and troubleshooting** answers the questions schools ask most often.
